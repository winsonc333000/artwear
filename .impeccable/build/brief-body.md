# Home (/)

Scope: ARTwear home page. Mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: collectors and fans arriving from an artist's social media; they want to see what one-of-one, artist-worn pieces are available and add one to the bag. Proof: the garments themselves (paint, wear) with provenance labels. Content is placeholder until real artists and photos arrive; all names and photos are marked as samples.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/draft-c-wall.png` (draft C), with the user's stated changes: the "Drop 03 / November" heading is removed entirely; the slogan "Authentic artist work clothes" sits as a tagline directly under the ARTwear wordmark; the hung garment and its label are centred on the page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home page is a bare gallery wall with one worn garment hung on it, labelled like an artwork, and the catalog below reads as the show's checklist. It refuses the streetwear drop template (campaign photo with a CTA button over a product grid).

OWN-WORLD: Gallery-white wall ground with soft wall shadow under hung pieces; black only for type and controls; colour comes only from paint on garments. Caesura-style high-contrast serif for display, Aktiv Grotesk-style neo-grotesk for everything else. Signature component: the tombstone wall label (artist / garment, wear / media / No. · 1 of 1 / Add to bag), pinned to the wall with corner pins. Sold = black dot + label "Sold".

STORY: The visitor sees that this is a real worn garment from a named artist, that it exists once, and can add it to the bag from the label; then scans the catalog by category.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Wordmark top-left (serif), nav right (Shop, Artists, Drop, About, Bag). Under the wordmark, small: the tagline "Authentic artist work clothes". No headline. Centre: one hung chore coat at about 45% of viewport height on a hanger and pin, horizontally centred on the page, with its wall label just to its right holding the Add to bag button; the rest of the wall stays empty. Lower band: category tabs (All, Tops, Bottoms, Outerwear, Aprons) and the first row of the catalog, five hung garments with numbered checklist captions.

FORM: Wall Label & Red Dot (pick card, quieted per user steer), position 1 on my ordered list; seed:c7a6c4d561615154.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
